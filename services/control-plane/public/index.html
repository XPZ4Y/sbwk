How it works

From request to root cause

1

Intercept

A container takes inbound traffic and forwards it to your server. Nothing reaches the app unread.

2

Detect

A built-in detector flags 404s, 500s and slow responses, then dispatches the core agent.

3

Diagnose

The agent pulls auth and database logs, then compares your codebase to recent commits.

4

Escalate

It proposes a fix. Below your confidence threshold, it stops and wakes the human engineer.
